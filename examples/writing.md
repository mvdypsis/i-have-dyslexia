# Writing examples

## 1. A quick message to a manager

The person writes:

> need to tell my boss the clinet meting moved to thursday and i cant make it becuase of the dentist, can you write it

Without the skill:

> Here's a draft. (Note: "clinet" should be "client", "meting" should be "meeting", and "becuase" should be "because".)

With the skill:

> Here's the message:
>
> Hi Rui,
> The client meeting has moved to Thursday. I can't make it, as I have a dentist appointment that day.
> Could someone else cover it, or can we find another time?
>
> Want it more formal or more casual?

## 2. Thoughts in any order

The person writes:

> ok so the project is late, its not our fault the supplier was late, but we can still do friday if they send it monday, i want to say sorry but not too much

With the skill:

> Here's what I think you want to say:
> the project is late because of the supplier, you can still deliver Friday if parts arrive Monday, and you want a light apology. Right?
>
> If yes, here's a draft:
>
> Hi all,
> Sorry for the delay on the project. Our supplier was late with the parts.
> If they arrive on Monday, we can still deliver on Friday. I'll confirm on Monday.
