# Visual examples

## 1. Comparing options

The person asks: **"should I take the train or drive to Porto"**

With the skill:

> **The train is better unless you need the car there.**
>
> | | Train | Car |
> |---|---|---|
> | Time | 3 h | 3 h 30 |
> | Cost | 30 € | 60 € with tolls |
> | Can work on the way | Yes | No |
>
> Look at the last row first. If you need to work, take the train.

## 2. Mapping a messy idea

The person says: **"I want to start a podcast but I don't know where to begin"**

With the skill, Claude asks for the big parts first, then shows the map:

```
                 Podcast
     ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
   Topic          Gear           Publish
   - Who for?     - Microphone   - Where?
   - Episode 1    - Quiet room   - How often?
```
