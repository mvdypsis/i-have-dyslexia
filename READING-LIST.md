# Inspired by the best

The strategies come from two places.
Some are shared by dyslexic people through the form.
Others are inspired by books and by dyslexic people who have spoken in public about how they think.

Every link below was opened and checked before it went in.
If a link breaks, or a claim looks wrong, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mvdypsis/i-have-dyslexia/issues/new/choose).

## Books

| Book | What it offers | Strategies |
|---|---|---|
| **The Dyslexic Advantage**, Brock and Fernette Eide (2011) | Four strengths they call MIND: Material, Interconnected, Narrative and Dynamic reasoning. A framework, not settled science. ([interview](https://dianerehm.org/shows/2012-02-15/dyslexic-brain)) | Build a model, Learn from cases, Run the movie forward |
| **Overcoming Dyslexia**, Sally Shaywitz (2003) | The science of reading, and why time and tools matter. ([publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/165314/overcoming-dyslexia-by-sally-shaywitz-md-and-jonathan-shaywitz-md/), [Yale Center](https://dyslexia.yale.edu/resources/accommodations/time-and-tools/)) | Read with your ears |
| **Thinking Differently**, David Flink (2014) | A guide by the founder of Eye to Eye, who has dyslexia and ADHD. ([Teachers College](https://www.tc.columbia.edu/articles/2021/march/hes-a-cnn-hero-alumnus-david-flink-views-ld-as-learning-differently/)) | Name what you need |
| **In the Mind's Eye**, Thomas G. West (1991) | Visual thinkers and dyslexia. Its historical examples include people not confirmed as dyslexic, so read it for the idea. | Think in pictures |
| **The Gift of Dyslexia**, Ronald D. Davis (1994) | Picture thinking and clay models. Its research is weak. ([method](https://www.dyslexia.com/davis-difference/about-davis/inside-the-davis-program/beating-dyslexia-with-clay/), [a critical view](https://www.spelfabet.com.au/2013/08/davis-dyslexia-method/)) | Build a model |

## People

| Person | What they do | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Ingvar Kamprad**, IKEA | Gave products names, not numbers, because names were easier to remember. ([IKEA](https://www.ikea.com/ae/en/cat/skadis-series-37813/)) | Name it, don't number it |
| **Richard Branson**, Virgin | Keeps messages simple, and delegates what he finds difficult. ([NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/lifestyle/richard-branson-talks-dyslexia-and-what-kids-like-him-really-need/7AJUKGXFBIROC3Z73NOWFCUUIY/)) | Keep the message simple, Build a team around your gaps |
| **Paul Orfalea**, Kinko's | Handed reading to others, and learned by walking the stores. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2002/05/13/the-dyslexic-ceo), [Edge Foundation](https://edgefoundation.org/paul-orfalea-turning-learning-challenges-into-business-opportunities/)) | Build a team around your gaps, Go and look |
| **Charles Schwab**, Schwab | Reaches conclusions fast, thinks in concepts and pictures, listens to audiobooks. ([Yale](https://www.dyslexia.yale.edu/story/charles-schwab/)) | Jump to the answer, then check, Think in pictures, Read with your ears |
| **Carol Greider**, Nobel Prize 2009 | Pulls information out of context and puts ideas together. ([Yale](https://dyslexia.yale.edu/story/carol-greider-ph-d/)) | Use the context |
| **John Irving**, novelist | Does not begin a novel until he knows the ending. ([Academy of Achievement](https://achievement.org/achiever/john-irving/)) | Start from the end |
| **Jamie Oliver**, chef | "Get up and do something." ([Child Mind Institute](https://childmind.org/blog/dyslexic-jamie-oliver-struggled-with-stigma/)) | Learn by doing |

## The frame

The six Dyslexic Thinking skills come from [Made By Dyslexia](https://www.madebydyslexia.org/).
In 2022, LinkedIn added Dyslexic Thinking as a skill people can list on their profile ([Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/magazine/entertainment/2022/03/30/news/richard-branson-hails-linkedin-for-adding-dyslexic-thinking-as-official-skill-2629602/)).

## What we leave out

Lists of famous dyslexic people often include Einstein, Edison or Leonardo da Vinci.
Their dyslexia is not confirmed, so they are not here.
