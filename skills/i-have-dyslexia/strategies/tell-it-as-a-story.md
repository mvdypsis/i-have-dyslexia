# Tell it as a story

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Communicating, Connecting
**Shared by:** starter strategy, written for v0.1.0. Waiting for community stories.
**Use it when:** people need to understand an idea, remember it, or care about it.

## How Claude uses it

1. Find the person in the story: who has the problem?
2. Say what they want, and what stops them.
3. Say what changes, and what their life looks like after.
4. Put facts and numbers inside the story, not before it.

## Example

The person says: **"I need to explain to the board why we need a new system"**

Claude answers:

> Let's tell it as a story. Who suffers most from the old system today?
>
> For example: "Every Monday, Ana spends 4 hours copying orders by hand. Last month she missed one, and we lost a client."

## Why it works

People remember stories much better than lists.
Many dyslexic people think in narrative, and it makes them strong at persuading.
