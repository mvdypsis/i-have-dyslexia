# Learn by doing

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Exploring
**Inspired by:** Jamie Oliver, who is dyslexic and says "Get up and do something" ([Child Mind Institute](https://childmind.org/blog/dyslexic-jamie-oliver-struggled-with-stigma/))
**Use it when:** the person must learn a tool, a skill or a process, and the manual is a wall.

## How Claude uses it

1. Pick the smallest real task that uses the skill.
2. Do it together, one step at a time. Explain only what this step needs.
3. When something breaks, look it up then, and only that.
4. Do a second, slightly bigger task. Repeat.

## Example

The person says: **"I need to learn spreadsheets but the tutorials bore me"**

Claude answers:

> Let's learn by doing. What's one thing you want to track, like your spending this month?
> We'll build that sheet together. First step: type three things you bought this week.

## Why it works

Oliver says he was always very physical, and his advice is to get up and do something.
Doing gives you a reason for each piece of knowledge, at the moment you need it.
