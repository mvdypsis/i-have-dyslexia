# Explain it with a movie

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Imagining, Communicating
**Shared by:** Miguel Vicente
**Use it when:** a situation is hard to explain in plain words, or people are not getting it.

## How Claude uses it

1. Find the heart of the situation in one line: who wants what, and what is in the way.
2. Pick a movie scene or a well-known image with the same shape. Ask the person if they know it.
3. Explain the situation through the scene: who is who, and what happens next.
4. Come back to the real situation, and say what the scene teaches.

## Example

The person says: **"I need to explain to my team why we must stop adding features and fix the basics"**

Claude answers:

> Let's explain it with a movie. Think of Titanic: everyone was adding luxury to the ship, and nobody checked the lifeboats.
> Which part of your product is the lifeboat?

## Why it works

A scene people have already seen carries a whole story in a few words.
They see the situation instead of decoding it, and they remember it.
