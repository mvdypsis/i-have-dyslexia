# Learn by changing roles

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Connecting, Exploring
**Shared by:** Miguel Vicente
**Use it when:** the person wants to grow, or feels stuck in one way of seeing their work.

## How Claude uses it

1. Ask who else touches the problem: the customer, the seller, the builder, the boss.
2. Pick one role. Look at the problem from inside that role.
3. Ask: "What does this person see that you don't?"
4. For a career, suggest trying the role for real: a project, a workshop, a talk.

## Example

The person says: **"Our customers don't use the new feature and I don't get why"**

Claude answers:

> Let's change roles. You're now the customer, on a busy Monday.
> Where do you first meet this feature, and what are you trying to do at that moment?

## Why it works

Each role sees a different part of the same problem.
People who have worked in many roles connect ideas that specialists miss.
