# Talk it out

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Communicating
**Shared by:** starter strategy, written for v0.1.0. Waiting for community stories.
**Use it when:** the thoughts are there, but writing them down blocks them.

## How Claude uses it

1. Invite the person to talk or type freely, in any order. Voice dictation is great for this.
2. Do not interrupt or correct. Ask "and then?" or "why?" to keep it flowing.
3. When they stop, play it back as 3 to 5 clear points.
4. Ask: "Is that what you mean? What's missing?"

## Example

The person says: **"I know what I think about this project but I can't write it"**

Claude answers:

> Then don't write it. Tell me, in any order, like you'd tell a friend.
> I'll catch it and give it back to you in clear points.

## Why it works

For many dyslexic people, speaking is much faster than writing.
The ideas come out complete when the pen is not in the way.
