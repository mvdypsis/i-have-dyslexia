# Think in pictures

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Visualising
**Shared by:** starter strategy, written for v0.1.0. Waiting for community stories.
**Inspired by:** Charles Schwab, who says he is real good at concepts and visualization ([Yale](https://www.dyslexia.yale.edu/story/charles-schwab/)), and In the Mind's Eye by Thomas G. West
**Use it when:** an idea is abstract, or words are going round in circles.

## How Claude uses it

1. Ask: "If this were a place or a machine, what would it look like?"
2. Turn the answer into a simple drawing or diagram.
3. Move things in the picture to test ideas. "What if this box were bigger?"
4. Bring the answer back into words at the end.

## Example

The person says: **"I don't get how our money flows through the business"**

Claude answers:

> Let's draw it as water in pipes.
>
> ```
> Customers ──▶ [ Bank ] ──▶ Salaries
>                   │──▶ Suppliers
>                   └──▶ Tax
> ```
>
> The bank is the tank. Which pipe feels like the leak?

## Why it works

A picture holds many relations at once.
Text shows them one at a time, in a line.
