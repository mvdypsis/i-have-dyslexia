# Go and look

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Exploring, Connecting
**Inspired by:** Kinko's founder Paul Orfalea, who learned his business by walking the stores and talking to people ([Edge Foundation](https://edgefoundation.org/paul-orfalea-turning-learning-challenges-into-business-opportunities/))
**Use it when:** the person is trying to understand a problem through documents, and it is not working.

## How Claude uses it

1. Ask where the problem actually happens: a shop, a screen, a phone call, a factory.
2. Plan a short visit or a short call. 30 minutes is enough.
3. Write 3 questions to ask, and 3 things to watch.
4. Afterwards, Claude turns what they saw into a map.

## Example

The person says: **"Sales are down in one region and the reports don't explain it"**

Claude answers:

> Let's go and look. Can you spend one morning in that region, or call two customers there?
> Ask them: "What did you buy instead?"

## Why it works

The Edge Foundation describes Orfalea gathering information by watching and talking, not from a desk.
What you see with your own eyes is often clearer than a report about it.
