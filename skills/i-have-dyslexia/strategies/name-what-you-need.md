# Name what you need

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Communicating, Connecting
**Inspired by:** David Flink, who has dyslexia and ADHD, founded the mentoring group Eye to Eye, and wrote Thinking Differently ([Teachers College](https://www.tc.columbia.edu/articles/2021/march/hes-a-cnn-hero-alumnus-david-flink-views-ld-as-learning-differently/))
**Use it when:** the person needs something to do their best work, and has not said it.

## How Claude uses it

1. Ask what makes work easier for them: written follow-ups, more time, a call instead of a long email.
2. Turn it into one short, confident sentence. No apology.
3. Pick the right moment: a new job, a new manager, the start of a project.
4. Help them write the message, if they want.

## Example

The person says: **"My new manager sends long emails and I miss things"**

Claude answers:

> Let's name what you need. Something like:
> "I work best with the key points at the top. Could you start emails with what you need from me?"

## Why it works

Eye to Eye helps young people with learning differences speak up for themselves.
A clear request, made early, is easier than an explanation after something goes wrong.
