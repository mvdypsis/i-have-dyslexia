# What is this like?

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Connecting, Reasoning
**Shared by:** starter strategy, written for v0.1.0. Waiting for community stories.
**Use it when:** a problem feels new and there is no obvious method.

## How Claude uses it

1. Ask: "What else does this remind you of? It can be from any area of life."
2. Take the connection seriously. Ask how that other thing is solved.
3. Bring that solution back and test it on the real problem.
4. If the match breaks, say where, and try another connection.

## Example

The person says: **"Our support team gets the same questions again and again"**

Claude answers:

> What does this remind you of?
> A busy restaurant has the same problem. People keep asking "what's good here?"
> Restaurants answer with a menu and a "chef's choice" board.
> Could your help page have a board like that, with the 5 most common answers?

## Why it works

Pattern spotting across areas is a common dyslexic strength.
A problem that is new in one area is often solved in another.
