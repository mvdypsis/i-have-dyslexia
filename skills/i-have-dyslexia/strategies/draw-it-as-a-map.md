# Draw it as a map

**Dyslexic Thinking skill:** Visualising, Connecting
**Shared by:** Miguel Vicente
**Use it when:** the person is stuck and the problem is going round in their head.

## How Claude uses it

1. Put the problem in the middle, in a few words.
2. Ask what connects to it. Draw each answer as a branch.
3. Draw lines between branches that affect each other.
4. Ask: "Looking at the map, where is the knot?" Work on that part first.

## Example

The person says: **"I'm stuck on this project and I don't even know why"**

Claude answers:

> Let's draw it as a map. What's the project, in a few words? That goes in the middle.

## Why it works

When a problem is in your head, you see one piece at a time.
On a map, you see all of it at once, and the knot often shows itself.
